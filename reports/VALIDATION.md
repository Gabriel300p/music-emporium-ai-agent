## Document ambiguity review

- Source manual lists contact details with discrepancies between page 2 (company contact) and page 6 (main WhatsApp channel). An agent should distinguish channels and refer ambiguous cases to the human team.
- Manual discusses a 30-day exchange procedure for manufacturing defects (page 4) and a 90-day legal warranty (page 7). They refer to distinct mechanisms; the assistant must not collapse them into one unconditional deadline or provide independent legal rulings.
- Active promotion status is a CSV boolean without date ranges; calling it "active in the snapshot" is justified, while asserting a current November 2026 campaign would not be.
